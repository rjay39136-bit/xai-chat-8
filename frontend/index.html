Help & Documentation

About XAI

XAI is an explainable AI assistant designed to deliver fast, transparent answers with rationale, document and image understanding, and comparative chat analysis.

Key Features

  • Ask Anything: Streaming responses powered by Google Gemini with live token delivery.
  • Upload & Analyze: Attach images (PNG, JPG) or code/documents (TXT, PDF) for visual & textual analysis.
  • Combine & Compare: Select any two past conversations to generate a comparative analysis of their answers.
  • Voice Input: Dictate prompts hands-free using the built-in speech recognition tool.
  • Light & Dark Mode: Seamless toggle with the Sun/Moon button in the top right.
